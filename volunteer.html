Share what you know

Support fellow UWTSD Computing students by explaining topics, sharing study strategies and helping build a welcoming peer-learning community.

Why volunteer?

Peer support can benefit both the student receiving help and the volunteer sharing their knowledge.

What volunteers can do

  • Explain topics from a Computing module you understand well.
  • Share useful revision methods and learning resources.
  • Help peers practise concepts through examples.
  • Encourage respectful, inclusive and independent learning.

Suggested eligibility

Volunteers should be willing to help, communicate clearly, respect other students and be honest about the topics they can support. Any real programme would need an agreed university process for eligibility and verification.
