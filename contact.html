About TutorBridge

Helping UWTSD Computing students learn together through peer support and free learning resources.

Who We Are

TutorBridge is a student support website designed for Computing students at the University of Wales Trinity Saint David.

It connects students who need academic support with volunteer students who can share their knowledge and experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make learning easier by encouraging students to support one another.

We provide access to peer tutoring, useful learning materials and opportunities for volunteering.

Our Objectives

  • Connect students with volunteer tutors.
  • Provide free online learning resources.
  • Encourage teamwork and peer learning.
  • Help students improve their Computing skills.
